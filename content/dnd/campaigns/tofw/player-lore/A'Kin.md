# A'Kin

*A merchant who deals in magical curiosities and rare items*

## What You Know

A'Kin is a merchant in Sigil who deals in magical curiosities and rare items—the kind of goods you won't find at your average bazaar stall. His shop is tucked away somewhere in the Market Ward, and if you're looking for something unusual, expensive, or just a little bit dangerous, A'Kin probably has it.

He's affable enough, with the smooth professionalism of someone who knows exactly what he's selling and what it's worth. He's not trying to cheat you (at least not obviously), but his prices reflect the rarity and power of his wares. If you've got the coin, or something interesting to trade, A'Kin is willing to deal.

His inventory recently included things like spell scrolls, rings of swimming and jumping, wands of web and fear, robes of eyes, horns of blasting, and even a tome of clear thought. If you need specialized magical equipment, A'Kin's shop is one of the best places in Sigil to find it.

In a city full of hustlers and cross-traders, A'Kin is refreshingly straightforward. He's a merchant, not a schemer.

A few of you owe him a favor, and eventually he'll call it in.
