# Parisa

*Your guide through Sigil's early days*

## What You Know

Parisa was your guide through Sigil's streets in those first confusing days after you escaped the Mortuary. She knows the city well—the wards, the factions, where to find what you need and who to avoid if you want to keep your skin intact.

She helped you navigate the overwhelming maze of the City of Doors, pointing out the landmarks, explaining the cant, and making sure you didn't walk headfirst into a portal to the Nine Hells. Her connections span the wards, and she seems to know people everywhere—or at least, she knows where to find them.

Parisa strikes a balance between being helpful and keeping her distance. She's not your friend, exactly, but she's not just a hired guide either. There's a sense that she's invested in seeing you figure things out, though whether that's genuine concern or professional interest is hard to say.

She was there for your early explorations—introducing you to the way Sigil works, the factions that control it, and the unspoken rules that keep the Cage from tearing itself apart. If you needed to know where to go or who to talk to, Parisa had the answer. Or at least, she knew someone who did.

You haven't seen much of her since you left Sigil for the Outlands, but in a city where knowing the right people is the difference between survival and the dead-book, Parisa is definitely one of the right people.
